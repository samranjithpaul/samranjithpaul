Place the beads on your canvas

  1. Measure the usable grid and enter its dimensions and cell spacing. The pattern rounds down to whole cells.
  2. Open the numbered placement view or download the PDF. The first PDF page shows the section map and bead counts. The placement pages show row and column numbers measured from the top-left corner.
  3. On A4, print at Actual size / 100% and measure the 50 mm ruler on a placement page. Align the first cell to the top-left of your canvas. Place each bead by its legend number.
